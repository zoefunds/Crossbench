## Current disposition

- Runtime writes are direct wallet-to-contract transactions.
- The backend is read-only and contains no signer or protocol authority.
- Claimant and intended respondent identities are wallet-bound.
- Policies name their issuer and require primary-source verification.
- On-chain evidence has structured chain, object-type, and object-value fields.
- Canonical URL, structured-ledger, and fetched-content duplicates are handled.
- Validator equality uses material decision fields, not complete page bytes.
- Autofill provides complete inputs for every category and lifecycle evidence
  form, with distinct challenge sources.
- Fixed-window lifecycle behavior, settlement, withdrawals, and accounting are
  covered by the direct contract suite.

## Known network limitation

StudioNet rolls back state when the outer consensus transaction fails. As a
result, a failure counter increment performed immediately before raising does
not persist on that network. The source and direct tests document the intended
no-consensus recovery flow, while `docs/ESCROW_RECOVERY.md` accurately records
which historical test escrows cannot currently reach its threshold.
